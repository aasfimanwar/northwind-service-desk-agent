# POL-INC: Incident Reporting Procedure

- Version: 1.8
- Effective date: 2026-01-20
- Owner: Security Operations
- Company: Northwind Ltd (fictional; synthetic training data)

### POL-INC-1 What to report

Report phishing that was clicked or opened, lost devices, suspected unauthorised access, and data sent to the wrong recipient.

### POL-INC-2.1 Reporting time

Report to IT Security within 1 hour of discovery, by email to security@northwind.example.

### POL-INC-2.2 Personal data

Suspected personal-data breaches are notified to the Data Protection Officer at once. The DPO decides whether the regulator must be told within 72 hours.

### POL-INC-3.1 Severity

Incidents are rated S1 (active compromise), S2 (probable compromise) or S3 (low risk).

### POL-INC-3.2 Service Desk role

The Service Desk does not investigate S1 or S2 incidents. It escalates them to Security Operations immediately.

### POL-INC-4 No blame

Reporting in good faith is never penalised.
