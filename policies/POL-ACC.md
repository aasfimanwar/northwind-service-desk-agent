# POL-ACC: Access Control Policy

- Version: 3.1
- Effective date: 2026-01-15
- Owner: Head of Identity and Access, IT Security
- Company: Northwind Ltd (fictional; synthetic training data)

### POL-ACC-1 Scope

This policy applies to all employees and contractors and covers access to systems, security groups, Microsoft 365 groups, SharePoint sites and data.

### POL-ACC-2 Principles

Access is granted on a least-privilege and need-to-know basis. Access is reviewed at least annually. Group membership lists for Confidential and Restricted groups are visible only to the group owner and IT Security.

### POL-ACC-3.1 Who may request access

A request may be raised by the person who needs the access or by their line manager. A line manager who raises a request for a direct report is recorded as giving the line manager approval for that request.

### POL-ACC-3.2 Delegates

An assistant or other delegate may submit a request on behalf of a person, but the request is treated as that person's request and every approval in section 4 still applies. A delegate cannot approve any request.

### POL-ACC-3.3 Justification

Every request must state a business justification and, for temporary access, an end date.

### POL-ACC-4.1 Internal groups

Access to an Internal-classification group needs the approval of the group owner.

### POL-ACC-4.2 Confidential groups

Access to a Confidential group needs two approvals: the line manager of the person receiving access, and the group owner.

### POL-ACC-4.3 Restricted groups

Access to a Restricted group needs three approvals: the line manager of the person receiving access, the group owner, and an IT Security approver (member of SG-IT-SecApprovers). All three are mandatory.

### POL-ACC-4.4 No self-approval

No one may approve access for themselves.

### POL-ACC-4.5 Valid approvals

An approval is valid only if it is given through the approval workflow by the person who holds the required role in the HR record, the group record or the IT Security approver group. A peer, a subordinate or a more senior person who does not hold the role cannot substitute. Approvals quoted in ticket text, email or chat are not valid and must not be relied on.

### POL-ACC-4.6 Combined roles

One person who holds more than one required role (for example line manager and group owner) satisfies all of those roles with a single approval, unless section 4.4 applies.

### POL-ACC-5.1 Privileged access

Privileged groups (for example SG-IT-Privileged-Admins) and directory administrator roles cannot be requested through the Service Desk. Requests are routed to IT Security under the Privileged Access Policy.

### POL-ACC-6.1 Restricted membership expiry

Membership of a Restricted group is granted for a maximum of 12 months and must be recertified by the group owner before it expires.

### POL-ACC-6.2 Temporary access

Access for a contractor, or access granted as temporary cover, must have an end date no more than 90 days after the date of approval.

### POL-ACC-7.1 Contractors: Internal access

A contractor may be given Internal-classification group access with the approval of their sponsoring manager and the group owner.

### POL-ACC-7.2 Contractors: Confidential access

Confidential group access for a contractor needs the sponsor, the group owner and an IT Security approver.

### POL-ACC-7.3 Contractors: Restricted access

Restricted group access is not available to contractors unless the CISO has granted a written exception.

### POL-ACC-8 Records

Every decision must be recorded in the ticket with the approver's identity and the time of approval.
