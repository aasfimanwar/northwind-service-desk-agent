# POL-ONB: Onboarding Checklist

- Version: 2.0
- Effective date: 2026-01-05
- Owner: HR Operations and IT Services
- Company: Northwind Ltd (fictional; synthetic training data)

### POL-ONB-1 Trigger

Onboarding starts when HR creates the joiner record with a start date. The account is created disabled and enabled on the start date.

### POL-ONB-2.1 Access pack request

The line manager requests the role-based access pack at least 5 working days before the start date. A late request is still processed but is flagged as late.

### POL-ONB-2.2 Standard packs

Standard packs are ENG-STD (SG-Eng-Platform-Dev and the Engineering team), FIN-STD (the Finance team site; Confidential finance groups are not included) and SAL-STD (SG-Sales-CRM-Users). Packs contain Internal-classification access only. Anything further follows the Access Control Policy.

### POL-ONB-3.1 Device

A configured device is delivered to the joiner at least 2 working days before the start date.

### POL-ONB-3.2 First sign-in

The Level 2 Service Desk issues a Temporary Access Pass on day 1 as described in the Conditional Access and Lockout Guide.

### POL-ONB-4.1 Mandatory training

Security Awareness, GDPR and Code of Conduct training must be completed within 14 days of the start date.

### POL-ONB-4.2 Training and access

Confidential and Restricted access is not granted to a joiner until the mandatory training is complete.

### POL-ONB-5 Contractors

A contractor's sponsor raises the request. An end date is mandatory and the engagement may not exceed 6 months without renewal.
