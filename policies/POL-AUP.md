# POL-AUP: Acceptable Use Policy

- Version: 3.2
- Effective date: 2026-02-10
- Owner: Head of Information Security
- Company: Northwind Ltd (fictional; synthetic training data)

### POL-AUP-1 Use of systems

Northwind systems are for business use. Limited personal use is allowed if it does not interfere with work or break any policy.

### POL-AUP-2.1 Personal storage

Company data must not be stored in personal cloud storage or sent to personal email accounts.

### POL-AUP-2.2 AI tools

Only AI tools on the approved list, currently Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat with enterprise data protection, may be used with company data. Confidential and Restricted data must not be entered into any other AI tool.

### POL-AUP-3 Software

Software is installed from Company Portal only. Standard users do not have administrator rights. Exceptions are decided by IT Security.

### POL-AUP-4 Monitoring

Northwind may monitor its business systems in line with its privacy notice.

### POL-AUP-5 Credentials

Never share your password or MFA codes. The Service Desk will never ask for them.
