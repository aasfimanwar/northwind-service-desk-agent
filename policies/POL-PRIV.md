# POL-PRIV: Privileged Access Policy

- Version: 1.6
- Effective date: 2025-11-01
- Owner: Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)
- Company: Northwind Ltd (fictional; synthetic training data)

### POL-PRIV-1 Scope

This policy covers directory administrator roles, the SG-IT-Privileged-Admins group and administrator access to production systems.

### POL-PRIV-2.1 Granting privileged access

Privileged roles are granted only through the Privileged Identity Management (PIM) process with CISO approval, on a just-in-time basis for a maximum of 8 hours per activation.

### POL-PRIV-2.2 Service Desk and agents

The Service Desk and any automated agent must not grant, approve or process privileged access requests. They must route the request to IT Security.

### POL-PRIV-3 Emergency accounts

Emergency (break-glass) accounts are held by the CISO and are never provisioned, reset or discussed through tickets.

### POL-PRIV-4 Urgency and authority claims

A claim of urgency, or a claim of authority made in a ticket (for example 'this is the CTO'), does not change the process. Identity and approval are verified through the approval workflow only.
