# POL-LEAVE: Leave Policy (UK)

- Version: 4.0
- Effective date: 2026-01-01
- Owner: People Team
- Company: Northwind Ltd (fictional; synthetic training data)

### POL-LEAVE-1 Scope

This policy applies to employees based in the United Kingdom. Employees in other countries follow their local leave terms, which are not part of this policy.

### POL-LEAVE-2.1 Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 25 days of annual leave a year plus public holidays. Public holidays follow the calendar of the nation where the employee works (England and Wales, Scotland or Northern Ireland).

### POL-LEAVE-2.2 Carry-over

The leave year runs from January to December. Up to 5 days may be carried over and must be used by 31 March.

### POL-LEAVE-2.3 Notice

Leave of up to 5 consecutive working days needs 1 week's notice. Leave of more than 5 consecutive working days needs 4 weeks' notice.

### POL-LEAVE-2.4 Approval

Leave is approved by the line manager. Leave of more than 15 consecutive working days also needs Director approval.

### POL-LEAVE-3 Buying leave

Employees may buy up to 5 additional days through salary sacrifice. The window is open in January only.

### POL-LEAVE-4.1 Reporting sickness

Employees must tell their line manager before the start of their working day on the first day of sickness.

### POL-LEAVE-4.2 Self-certification

Employees may self-certify sickness for up to 7 calendar days. From day 8 a fit note from a GP is required.

### POL-LEAVE-4.3 Sick pay

After 6 months' service the company pays 4 weeks of sick pay at full salary, then Statutory Sick Pay.

### POL-LEAVE-5.1 Maternity

Enhanced maternity pay is 26 weeks at full pay, followed by statutory pay.

### POL-LEAVE-5.2 Paternity

Paternity leave is 2 weeks at full pay.

### POL-LEAVE-5.3 Shared parental leave

Shared parental leave follows the statutory rules. Employees should contact the People Team to plan it.

### POL-LEAVE-6 Bereavement

Up to 5 days of paid leave are available on the death of an immediate family member.
