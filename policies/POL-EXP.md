# POL-EXP: Expenses Policy

- Version: 3.3
- Effective date: 2026-04-01
- Owner: Finance Operations
- Company: Northwind Ltd (fictional; synthetic training data)

### POL-EXP-1 Principles

Expenses must be reasonable, have a clear business purpose and be supported by evidence.

### POL-EXP-2.1 Claim deadline

Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense. Late claims need Finance approval.

### POL-EXP-2.2 Receipts

A receipt is required for any single item over 25 pounds (GBP 25).

### POL-EXP-3.1 Travel class

Use standard class rail. Fly economy for flights under 6 hours and premium economy for flights of 6 hours or more. Business class needs CFO approval.

### POL-EXP-3.2 Hotels

Hotel limits are GBP 180 a night in London and GBP 130 a night elsewhere in the UK. Limits for hotels outside the UK follow the Finance rate card, which is not included in this policy.

### POL-EXP-3.3 Mileage

Business mileage in a private car is paid at 45 pence a mile for the first 10,000 business miles in a tax year and 25 pence a mile after that, in line with HMRC approved mileage rates.

### POL-EXP-4.1 Subsistence

Meals while travelling overnight are reimbursed up to GBP 25 a day.

### POL-EXP-4.2 Alcohol

Alcohol is not reimbursable, except as part of client entertainment that has been pre-approved.

### POL-EXP-4.3 Client entertainment

Client entertainment is pre-approved by the line manager up to GBP 50 a head.

### POL-EXP-5.1 Approval limits

The line manager approves claims up to GBP 1,000. A Director approves claims above GBP 1,000 and up to GBP 5,000. The Finance Director approves claims above GBP 5,000.

### POL-EXP-5.2 Own claims

No one may approve their own expense claim.

### POL-EXP-6.1 Not reimbursable

Fines (including parking and speeding fines), personal items and gifts over GBP 50 without prior approval are not reimbursable.

### POL-EXP-6.2 Homeworking allowance

Employees who work from home regularly receive a homeworking allowance of GBP 6 a week, in line with HMRC guidance.
