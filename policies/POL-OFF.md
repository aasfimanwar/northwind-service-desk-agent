# POL-OFF: Offboarding Checklist

- Version: 2.1
- Effective date: 2026-01-05
- Owner: HR Operations and IT Services
- Company: Northwind Ltd (fictional; synthetic training data)

### POL-OFF-1 Trigger

The offboarding checklist starts only when a matching HR leaver record with a last working day exists. A manager's request without an HR record is redirected to HR.

### POL-OFF-2.1 Account disable

The account is disabled at 18:00 on the last working day and active sessions are revoked.

### POL-OFF-2.2 Groups and licences

Group memberships and licences are removed within 1 working day after the account is disabled.

### POL-OFF-3.1 OneDrive handover

OneDrive content is retained for 90 days after the last working day. The line manager may request access for handover within that period.

### POL-OFF-3.2 Mailbox

The mailbox is retained for 90 days and can be delegated to the line manager on request within that period.

### POL-OFF-4 Devices

Devices must be returned within 5 working days after the last working day.

### POL-OFF-5.1 Legal hold

If the HR record carries a legal hold flag, no data may be deleted or released. Legal must approve any release. The Service Desk does not delete or release held data.

### POL-OFF-6.1 Immediate departures

Disabling an account before 18:00 on the last working day needs written instruction from the People Director or Employee Relations together with Security. A line manager's request is not sufficient. The Service Desk escalates to Security Operations.
