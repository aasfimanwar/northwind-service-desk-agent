# POL-DATA: Data Classification Policy

- Version: 2.0
- Effective date: 2025-09-01
- Owner: Data Protection Officer
- Company: Northwind Ltd (fictional; synthetic training data)

### POL-DATA-1 Labels

Northwind uses four classification labels: Public, Internal, Confidential and Restricted.

### POL-DATA-2.1 Internal

Internal data is general business information that is not intended for the public.

### POL-DATA-2.2 Confidential

Confidential data includes contracts, customer data, unreleased financial results and source code.

### POL-DATA-2.3 Restricted

Restricted data includes payroll, health data, legally privileged material, merger and acquisition material, and credentials or keys.

### POL-DATA-3.1 Applying labels

Data owners apply labels. Users must not lower a label. Changing a label needs the approval of the data owner. The Service Desk cannot relabel content.

### POL-DATA-3.2 Personal data

Personal data about customers or staff is at least Confidential.

### POL-DATA-4 Restricted handling

Restricted data must stay in Northwind-managed storage and must not be emailed to personal addresses.

### POL-DATA-5 Retention

Confidential records are kept for 6 years and Restricted records for 7 years unless a legal hold applies.
