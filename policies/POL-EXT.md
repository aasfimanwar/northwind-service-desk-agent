# POL-EXT: External Sharing Policy

- Version: 2.4
- Effective date: 2026-02-01
- Owner: Head of Information Security
- Company: Northwind Ltd (fictional; synthetic training data)

### POL-EXT-1 Scope

This policy applies to files, folders, sites and Teams shared with people outside Northwind (guests).

### POL-EXT-2.1 Anyone links

Anonymous 'Anyone with the link' sharing is disabled for all content. The Service Desk cannot enable it.

### POL-EXT-2.2 Personal email domains

Sharing with personal email addresses (for example Gmail, Outlook.com or Yahoo) is not permitted.

### POL-EXT-3.1 Internal content

Internal-classification content may be shared with a named guest from an approved partner domain after approval by the site owner.

### POL-EXT-3.2 Sponsors and expiry

Every guest must have a Northwind employee as sponsor. Guest accounts expire 90 days after creation unless the sponsor renews them.

### POL-EXT-4.1 Confidential content

Sharing Confidential content externally needs approval from the site owner and from Information Security, and a reference to a signed non-disclosure agreement (NDA) recorded in the ticket.

### POL-EXT-4.2 Confidential sharing limits

Confidential content is shared view-only, with named individuals only, and the share must expire within 30 days.

### POL-EXT-5.1 Restricted content

Restricted content must not be shared externally. Exceptions need the written approval of the General Counsel and the CISO and cannot be processed by the Service Desk.

### POL-EXT-6.1 Approved partner domains

The approved partner domains are contoso-partners.example, fabrikam-consulting.example and tailspin-logistics.example.

### POL-EXT-6.2 Other domains

A guest from any domain that is not on the approved list needs a domain review by Information Security before anything is shared.

### POL-EXT-7.1 Site sharing settings

Changes to a site's external sharing setting are made by Information Security only, not by the Service Desk.
